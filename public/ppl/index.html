01 · For the Player · Player Hub

Every player's career, building in real time

What every athlete sees the moment they step on court. Score is context. Earnings, audience growth, and sponsor triggers are the headlines — because that's what turns a match into a career.

Design decisions
Name over score. The hero element is the player, not the match. Match data is framed as context to their night, not the other way around. Earnings are live. The yellow $24,500 card grows in real time as sets are won and sponsor bonuses trigger — the economic reality of being a pro athlete, made visible. Sponsors are incentive structure, not branding wallpaper. Each brand partner shows what the player unlocks, not just a logo.
02 · For the Referee · Live Scorer

One screen. Two taps per point. The night runs on this.

Every PPL match has one referee and one tablet. This is what they live in for 90 minutes. Two giant tap targets, a center frame that auto-detects Golden Point at 40-40, and a single "Live to broadcaster" toggle that sends the scorer feed to every stream.

Design decisions
Golden Point auto-framed. At 40-40 the center card changes state — referee doesn't remember the rule, the app does. Zero training overhead for the court staff pool. Offline-first, always. Every tap persists locally and syncs when connectivity returns. A Wi-Fi hiccup at Arena 3 never loses a point. One stream, everywhere. The same tap that scores the point lights up the PadelNachos feed and the broadcast overlay. No plugin, no manual entry, no duplication.
03 · For the Operator · League Command

Three questions, answered at a glance

What every league operator checks on a tournament night: what's happening now, what players are earning, and what needs my attention. One screen. No scrolling for the answer.

Design decisions
Tonight's player payout is the hero KPI. Not viewership. Not sponsor count. The league's philosophy says players-first — so that's the number at the top, in padel yellow. The earnings distribution chart is the philosophy, visualised. $8K floor / $19K median / $42K top proves the league isn't making one star rich — it's building 40 careers.
04 · For the Operator · Operations Detail

When the glance isn't enough

The same platform, deeper view. Every court, every marquee match, every pending decision in one frame. For the ops lead running the control room when four matches overlap.

Design decisions
F1 timing-tower inspiration. Four courts stacked vertically, color-leading bars at each card's edge signal status (live / queued / turnover). An operator scans 4 arenas in 2 seconds. Decisions-first, not metrics-first. Sponsor bonus payouts, medical timeouts, media requests — the real workflow a tournament day runs on, currently duct-taped across Slack + email + spreadsheets.
05 · For the Analyst · Match Analytics

Every point generates story — automatically

The moment the match ends, coaches, commentators, and content teams have their report. Win probability across the match, Golden Point breakdown, head-to-head metrics — all derived from the referee's tap log. No video tagging, no manual stats team, no delay.

Design decisions
Earnings is the hero KPI — same number the player saw in Section 01. The $24,500 in the player's pocket and the $24,500 in the coach's report are one event stream, not two. Golden Point breakdown tells the story of the match. 7 of 8 Golden Points to Chingotto/Tapia is why the match ended 2-1, not the rally count. Zero video required. Every metric is derived from the referee's tap log — works on the PPL arenas that don't have broadcast cameras, works on the ones that do.

This is what we'd build
in year one.

These five surfaces are a thesis, not a product. They're meant to show what's possible when someone builds for PPL, not a generic league template retrofitted from tennis software. If we're aligned on the philosophy, the product falls out of that conversation.

What I'd propose
  • Design partnership for Year 1. We build PPL's platform together — you shape the roadmap, we do the engineering. Rate structured to reflect the partnership, not a vendor transaction.
  • Player-first by contract. Every feature we ship in Year 1 answers the same question: does this make PPL athletes richer, more visible, or better supported?
  • Exclusive in the US market for the first 24 months. You get first-mover advantage, we get a lighthouse partner to build the international version around.
  • PPL keeps its data and its brand. Platform is infrastructure. Your players, your league, your IP — always.
